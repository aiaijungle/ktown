FAQ

How do you read “해운대” in Korean?

“해운대” is read “hae-un-dae”. It means “Haeundae Beach”. Haeundae is Busan's most famous beach and a top travel destination.

How is “해운대” pronounced?

Pronounce it syllable by syllable: 해 (hae) - 운 (un) - 대 (dae), which together sounds like “hae-un-dae”.
