FAQ

How do you read “돼지국밥” in Korean?

“돼지국밥” is read “dwae-ji-guk-bap”. It means “Pork & rice soup”. Dwaeji-gukbap is Busan's signature dish: pork broth with rice. Found all over the city.

How is “돼지국밥” pronounced?

Pronounce it syllable by syllable: 돼 (dwae) - 지 (ji) - 국 (guk) - 밥 (bap), which together sounds like “dwae-ji-guk-bap”.
