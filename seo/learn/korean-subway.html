FAQ

How do you read “지하철” in Korean?

“지하철” is read “ji-ha-cheol”. It means “Subway”. Jihacheol means subway — Korea's fast, cheap way to get around every major city.

How is “지하철” pronounced?

Pronounce it syllable by syllable: 지 (ji) - 하 (ha) - 철 (cheol), which together sounds like “ji-ha-cheol”.
