FAQ

How do you read “올림픽홀” in Korean?

“올림픽홀” is read “ol-lim-pik-hol”. It means “Olympic Hall (Seoul)”. Olympic Hall in Seoul's Olympic Park hosts many concerts and K-pop showcases.

How is “올림픽홀” pronounced?

Pronounce it syllable by syllable: 올 (ol) - 림 (lim) - 픽 (pik) - 홀 (hol), which together sounds like “ol-lim-pik-hol”.
