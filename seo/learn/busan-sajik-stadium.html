FAQ

How do you read “사직운동장” in Korean?

“사직운동장” is read “sa-jik-un-dong-jang”. It means “Sajik Stadium (Busan)”. Sajik Stadium in Busan is home to the Lotte Giants baseball team and hosts big concerts and events.

How is “사직운동장” pronounced?

Pronounce it syllable by syllable: 사 (sa) - 직 (jik) - 운 (un) - 동 (dong) - 장 (jang), which together sounds like “sa-jik-un-dong-jang”.
