FAQ

How do you read “비빔밥” in Korean?

“비빔밥” is read “bi-bim-bap”. It means “Bibimbap”. Bibimbap is mixed rice with vegetables, egg, and gochujang — one of Korea's most famous dishes.

How is “비빔밥” pronounced?

Pronounce it syllable by syllable: 비 (bi) - 빔 (bim) - 밥 (bap), which together sounds like “bi-bim-bap”.
