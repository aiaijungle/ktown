FAQ

How do you read “서면” in Korean?

“서면” is read “seo-myeon”. It means “Seomyeon (downtown Busan)”. Seomyeon is Busan's busiest downtown — shopping, street food, and nightlife all in one place.

How is “서면” pronounced?

Pronounce it syllable by syllable: 서 (seo) - 면 (myeon), which together sounds like “seo-myeon”.

What does “서면” mean in English?

“서면” (seo-myeon) means “Seomyeon (downtown Busan)”. Seomyeon is Busan's busiest downtown — shopping, street food, and nightlife all in one place.
