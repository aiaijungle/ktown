FAQ

How do you read “아메리카노” in Korean?

“아메리카노” is read “a-me-ri-ka-no”. It means “Americano (coffee)”. Amerikano is the most-ordered coffee in Korea — just say it at any cafe counter.

How is “아메리카노” pronounced?

Pronounce it syllable by syllable: 아 (a) - 메 (me) - 리 (ri) - 카 (ka) - 노 (no), which together sounds like “a-me-ri-ka-no”.
