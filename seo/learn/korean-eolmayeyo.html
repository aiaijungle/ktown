FAQ

How do you read “얼마예요” in Korean?

“얼마예요” is read “eol-ma-ye-yo”. It means “How much is it?”. Eolmayeyo means 'how much is it?' — what you say to ask a price in any Korean shop.

How is “얼마예요” pronounced?

Pronounce it syllable by syllable: 얼 (eol) - 마 (ma) - 예 (ye) - 요 (yo), which together sounds like “eol-ma-ye-yo”.
