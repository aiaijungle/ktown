FAQ

How do you read “주세요” in Korean?

“주세요” is read “ju-se-yo”. It means “Please give me”. Juseyo means 'please give me' — the essential word for ordering food or shopping in Korea.

How is “주세요” pronounced?

Pronounce it syllable by syllable: 주 (ju) - 세 (se) - 요 (yo), which together sounds like “ju-se-yo”.
