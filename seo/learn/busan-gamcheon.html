FAQ

How do you read “감천문화마을” in Korean?

“감천문화마을” is read “gam-cheon-mun-hwa-ma-eul”. It means “Gamcheon Culture Village”. Gamcheon Culture Village is Busan's colorful hillside neighborhood, a famous photo spot for travelers.

How is “감천문화마을” pronounced?

Pronounce it syllable by syllable: 감 (gam) - 천 (cheon) - 문 (mun) - 화 (hwa) - 마 (ma) - 을 (eul), which together sounds like “gam-cheon-mun-hwa-ma-eul”.
